# PTDXF Export Rules

The converter shall transfer only visible information into PTDXF.

---

## Exportable Elements

Allowed:

- Visible text
- Visible images
- Visible page structure

---

## Non-Exportable Elements

Shall be removed:

- Hyperlinks
- Metadata
- Comments
- Scripts
- Macros
- Embedded files
- Revision history
- Forms

---

## Visibility Principle

Content not visible to a reader shall not be exported.

---

## Layout Preservation

The converter should preserve:

- Reading order
- Page order
- Relative positioning

without introducing active content.

---

## Output Requirement

Generated PTDXF documents shall pass validation without modification.
