# Why PTDXF?

# Why another document format?

This is one of the most common questions regarding PTDXF.

The purpose of PTDXF is not to replace PDF, DOCX or ODT.

The purpose of PTDXF is to solve a different problem:

Trusted document exchange.

---

## The Problem

Modern document formats are increasingly complex.

Many document formats may contain:

- Hyperlinks
- Metadata
- Embedded files
- Forms
- Scripts
- External references
- Active content

While these features are useful, they increase both complexity and attack surface.

Organizations often need a format that contains only the information a human can see.

---

## The PTDXF Approach

PTDXF follows a simple rule:

> What would not be visible on a printed document shall neither be stored nor displayed in a PTDXF document.

Only visible content is preserved.

Examples:

- Text
- Images
- Document layout

Everything else is either removed or rejected.

---

## Why Not Simply Use PDF?

PDF was designed primarily for document fidelity.

PTDXF was designed primarily for:

- Security
- Auditability
- Compliance
- Trusted Exchange

The goals are different.

---

## Why Not Simply Use PDF/A?

PDF/A focuses on preservation.

PTDXF focuses on:

- Preservation
- Security
- Validation
- Trusted exchange

PTDXF applies additional restrictions.

---

## PTDXF Goals

### Reduce Attack Surface

Removing active content reduces risk.

### Improve Auditability

Allowed content is intentionally limited.

### Improve Trust

Documents are validated before display.

### Simplify Analysis

The format is intentionally simple.

---

## Positioning

PTDXF complements existing formats.

A typical workflow:

DOCX → PDF → PTDXF

Create → Publish → Secure Exchange

---

## Conclusion

PTDXF is designed for environments where trust, security and compliance matter more than advanced document functionality.

---

## Motto

Visible Information. Nothing More.
