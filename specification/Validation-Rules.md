# PTDXF Validation Rules

## VR-001

Only allowed object types shall exist.

Allowed:

- Page
- Text
- Image

---

## VR-002

Only approved image formats may be used.

Allowed:

- JPEG
- PNG

---

## VR-003

Embedded executable content is prohibited.

---

## VR-004

Hyperlinks must not exist.

---

## VR-005

External resources must not exist.

---

## VR-006

Metadata must be removed.

---

## VR-007

Unknown object types shall trigger LEVEL 2.

---

## VR-008

Invalid document structures shall trigger LEVEL 2.

---

## VR-009

Failed integrity verification shall trigger LEVEL 2.
