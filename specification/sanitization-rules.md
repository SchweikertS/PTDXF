# PTDXF Sanitization Rules

## Purpose

Define how source documents are cleaned during PTDXF creation.

---

## Hyperlinks

Action:

Remove hyperlink targets.

Preserve:

Visible text only.

---

## Metadata

Action:

Remove metadata.

Examples:

- Author
- Company
- Revision
- GPS Location

---

## Embedded Files

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## Comments

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## Revision History

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## Scripts

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## Macros

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## External References

Action:

Remove completely.

---

## Active Content

Action:

Remove completely.
